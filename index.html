You can view and download the accounts. Only Admins can edit, upload, or delete them.

▸Download

Download template gives an empty Excel file with the column headers, two example rows, and an "Allowed values" sheet. Download accounts gives every account as it is now, in the same columns plus an id column, ready to edit and upload again.

▸Upload a file

Upload an Excel (or CSV) file in the template's columns. A row with an id (from Download accounts) updates that account; a row without one adds a new account. A blank cell keeps the account's current value. Accounts not in the file stay as they are. You'll see a preview before anything is saved. If a pick-list cell (asset class, owner, beneficiary, custodian, liquidity, retirement) has a value that isn't on the list, the preview asks you to accept it (adding it to the list), change it, or reject the upload. In the retirement column, "none" clears it.

▸Accounts

An account with a ticker is valued as units × the latest price; one without a ticker uses the value you enter ($ thousands) and its assumed annual return. Liquidity defaults to Liquid when there's a ticker or the asset class is Cash. Beneficiary fills in from the Account owner. Clear "Show" to gray a fund out on the Portfolio tab.

Numbers are rounded; click one to see or edit the exact value.

▸Target Asset Allocation

Your target share of total assets for each asset class, shown on Portfolio slide 3. Enter percentages (e.g. 44 for 44%); they must add up to 100% before you can save. Only Admins can change these.

▸Compare with Money

Upload Microsoft Money's Portfolio Value by Investment Account report and/or its Net Worth report (exported to Excel or CSV; choose both at once to check everything together) to find differences from the accounts here. Funds with a ticker are compared by units (Money's Quantity); accounts without a ticker by value. Each Money line is matched to one or more accounts once, and the match is remembered. Nothing in the accounts changes. The file itself isn't saved.

▸Delete all accounts

Removes every account. An Excel backup of all accounts downloads first, and you'll be asked to type DELETE. Prices and user sign-ins aren't affected. To restore, upload the backup file.

Returns on illiquid investments from their dated cashflows ($ thousands; negative = cash invested, positive = cash returned). IRR is annualized from the actual dates (like Excel's XIRR), counting the current value as cash received on the valuation date. MOIC = (cash returned + current value) ÷ cash invested. A held investment is linked to its illiquid account on the Setup tab, which supplies its current value; an investment with no account is no longer held and has no current value. Click a row to see and edit its cashflows. Only Admins can change these.

▾Tax liability

Estimated taxes due if every account (except Cash) were sold or withdrawn today, at the rates in Tax rates below. Click a type to see its accounts.

▸Calculations

One row per account, Cash excluded. Gain = current value − cost basis (from the Setup tab); Tax due = Gain × the rate for the account's type (Retirement: Roth or Traditional; otherwise non-retirement capital gains). A loss gives a negative tax due, which offsets the others.

▸Tax rates

The tax rate that applies to each kind of account, used to estimate tax liabilities. Enter percentages (e.g. 24 for 24%). Only Admins can change these.
